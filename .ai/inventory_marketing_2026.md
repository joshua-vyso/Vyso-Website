| Route | Status | Notes |

| `/learn*`, `/resources*` | **MUST KEEP** (content/SEO asset) | Feeds `/llms.txt`, internal linking, glossary |

Safe to remove entirely without touching Finch pages if the redesign drops the Orbit product; equally safe to keep untouched. |

| `/login`, `/onboarding` | **DO NOT TREAT AS MARKETING** | Auth-gated platform gateways living outside `app/app` for routing reasons only; `noindex`; deleting/restructuring these breaks the product, not the marketing site |

| Component | Status |
|---|---|
