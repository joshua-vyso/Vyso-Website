### D2. Transactional-vs-boilerplate evidence (three layers, ALL of them)
- LAYER 1 — input scope: `detectOrderAmendment` gains an explicit `sourceKind: 'message' | 'formal_document'` (or the call sites pass pre-scoped text): for attachment-sourced orders (pdf/image/html source), the detector receives ONLY message-context text (email subject + email BODY text — thread the actual body through customerEvidence or a dedicated field; microsoft-message-order.ts must stop passing the attachment's own normalized text as messageText for detection purposes) and NEVER the formal document's own prose or reader-absorbed order_notes. A formal document can still declare its own state via explicit STATUS evidence (below). Email-body-sourced orders keep today's inputs.
- LAYER 2 — cue hardening (order-amendment.ts, pure, tested): a cancellation/hold/amendment cue match is DISCARDED when its surrounding window (~120 chars before) contains rights/conditional boilerplate markers: /\b(reserves?\s+(?:the\s+)?(?:absolute\s+)?right|right\s+to|may\s+(?:be\s+)?cancel|shall\s+(?:be\s+)?cancel|in\s+the\s+event\s+of|entitled\s+to|option\s+to|failure\s+(?:on|of|to)|if\s+the\s+(?:vendor|supplier|purchaser))\b/i. Positive transactional patterns added: /status\s*[:\-]?\s*cancell?ed/i, /\bhas\s+been\s+cancell?ed\b/i, /\bplease\s+cancel\b/i, /\bdo\s+not\s+deliver\b/i.
- LAYER 3 — gate repair: `explicitChangeLanguage` is no longer satisfied by the cue's mere existence for cancellation/hold when lineCount > 0. New rule: with line items present AND a requested delivery date AND no TRANSACTIONAL cancellation evidence (the positive patterns above), the verdict is new_order — a positive "status: accepted/…accepted" marker strengthens it; a transactional cancellation marker (positive patterns) overrides regardless of lines. Delivery-date/quantity/address amendments on zero-line messages behave as today (PO 144583 regression must stay green).
- BOILERPLATE SECTIONING (lightweight): pure helper `stripBoilerplateSections(text)` — truncates/tags text at legal-section markers (/\b(terms\s+(?:and|&)\s+conditions|conditions\s+of\s+purchase|general\s+conditions|indemnit|warrant(?:y|ies)|governing\s+law)\b/i heading-ish occurrences) for CUE DETECTION ONLY (stored text/reader source unchanged). Used by the detector on whatever text it does receive; documented as heuristic, fail-open (no marker → full text).
- Reader prompt (order-prompt.ts, one clause): order_notes = the CURRENT order's instructions only — never terms and conditions, legal clauses, indemnities, or rights language; a formal document's T&C section is not notes.
