# Case-study note — Turn 'n Slice

The paragraph to send **alongside** `https://vyso.co.za/case-studies/turn-n-slice`
when a prospect asks "who else is using this?". The page carries the story; this
note is the covering line that says what the engagement was actually like.

**It is deliberately qualitative.** The public page contains no operational
figures — no rand amounts, no percentages, no counts of invoices or suppliers —
so this note contains none either. Anything numeric would be new disclosure about
a real customer's business and needs Turn 'n Slice's permission first (see
"Before you send", below).

---

## The paragraph (≈150 words)

> Turn 'n Slice is a Johannesburg food business and our first founding customer,
> and most of what Finch does now exists because of how they work. We started
> the way we start with everyone: a week inside their actual paperwork — price
> lists, customer accounts, orders, invoices, payments — asking where the same
> information was being retyped and where a decision was being made without the
> number that should have informed it. Building a customer price list turned out
> to be the sharpest example: a routine task that took real time and went stale
> the moment a cost moved. That is now seconds, and the pricing, the account and
> the invoice trail all read from the same place instead of three. They are
> replacing QuickBooks as their invoicing system, in stages, while trading. The
> useful lesson for you is the sequencing: nothing was switched on speculatively.
> The audit decided the order.

## Before you send

- [ ] **The paragraph above needs no permission** — every claim in it is already
      public on `/case-studies/turn-n-slice` (Johannesburg food business, founding
      customer, OrderFlow replacing QuickBooks, price lists in seconds, one
      connected operation) or is a statement about **our** method, not their
      numbers.
- [ ] **Ask Roberto before adding ANY of the following**, and get it in writing:
  - any rand figure, percentage, or saving attributed to Turn 'n Slice;
  - counts — invoices, suppliers, customers, SKUs, staff, locations;
  - what the Operations Audit specifically found in their business;
  - anything about their suppliers, their supplier prices, or their customers;
  - a screenshot of their workspace, or any Turn 'n Slice data on a screen;
  - a new quote, or an edit to the existing one. The quote on the public page is
    already cleared and is byte-identical to what he approved — **use it as-is or
    not at all**.
- [ ] If the prospect asks for a reference call, ask Roberto first. Do not offer
      one on the call.
- [ ] Never pair this note with a demo of Turn 'n Slice's workspace. The demo org
      is **Meridian Food Co.** and only Meridian (`docs/demo-runbook.md` §0).
